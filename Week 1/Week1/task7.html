How to Make Ramen

What You Need

  • 1 ramen packet
  • 500 ml water
  • 1 egg
  • Green onions
  • A cooking pot

Steps

  1. Boil the water in a pot.
  2. Add the noodles and cook for exactly 3 minutes.
  3. Add the seasoning packet and stir vigorously.
  4. Add the egg and green onions.
  5. Serve the ramen immediately.
