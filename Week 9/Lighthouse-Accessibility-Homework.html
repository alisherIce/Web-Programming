WEEK 09-1 · HOMEWORK 01

Lighthouse Accessibility Audit.

Run an accessibility audit on your own team project, remediate at least three issues, and document real before-and-after evidence.

Evidence note: This report is a working template. Enter your own Lighthouse scores and attach screenshots after running the audit. No scores are pre-filled.

Audit details

Audit and remediation checklist

  1. Open Chrome DevTools → Lighthouse → Accessibility and run the audit on the project URL.
  2. Save a screenshot of the initial score and failing audits.
  3. Choose at least three fixable issues from the report.
  4. Fix the source HTML/CSS. Common fixes include descriptive image alt text, associated form labels, sufficient color contrast, semantic buttons, and landmarks.
  5. Run Lighthouse again under the same conditions and save the new screenshot.

Record at least three issues

Submission evidence

  • Before screenshot: score and failing audit details.
  • Before/after code snippets for each fix.
  • After screenshot: new accessibility score.
  • Brief reflection on the changes.

Use Print → Save as PDF after adding your measured results and screenshots to this report.
